Know The Road Before You Start

The complete interior journey, stage by stage.

Every home you love went through the same seven stages. Knowing them upfront is what separates a calm build from a chaotic one.

  1. 01

    Planning

    Brief, lifestyle map, and budget frame. Decisions on paper before decisions in wood.

  2. 02

    Design

    Layouts, 3D walkthroughs, material and finish selections — the whole house, resolved.

  3. 03

    Approval

    Costing, drawings and scope signed off. No surprises once the site opens.

  4. 04

    Materials

    Procurement from vetted vendors. What lands on site is what was chosen — no swaps.

  5. 05

    Execution

    On-site build with a dedicated project lead. Weekly reviews and milestone photos.

  6. 06

    Quality Check

    Snag list, finish audit and function testing before you ever step in.

  7. 07

    Handover

    Keys, care guide and a walk-through. A home that's ready to be lived in, not fixed.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Still unsure? Reserve your seat and ask it live during the Q&A.

Who is this session for?

Homeowners at any point in their journey — whether you're still picking a builder, halfway through design conversations, or already living with regrets from a previous project. If you're about to spend a significant sum on your interiors, this hour is for you.

Is the session recorded? Will I get a replay?

No. This is a fully live session — nothing is recorded, and no replay is shared afterwards. To learn the framework and take part in the Q&A, you’ll need to be on the call at the time it runs. Block the slot on your calendar before you register, and turn up.

How long is the session?

One hour, start to finish. The framework and the live Q&A both sit inside that same hour — there is no separate Q&A afterwards. Come with your questions ready so you can drop them in when the moment fits.

Do I need to be looking to hire someone?

No. There is no sales pitch and no obligation. The framework works whether you're doing the project yourself, working with an architect, or hiring a full-service studio.

Why is there a ₹99 fee?

Because a small skin-in-the-game filter keeps the room serious. Live Q&A is only useful when the people in it actually turn up and ask real questions.
